# ISOCUBE — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 30, 2026

ISOCUBE is a fully offline game. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- **No data collection:** The app runs entirely on your device. It does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs that track you.
- **No accounts:** You don't need to sign in or create an account to play.
- **No internet use:** The game works completely offline; it makes no network requests.
- **No permissions:** The app does not access your location, camera, contacts, or any device sensors.
- **Children's privacy:** Because no data is collected, this app is suitable for all ages.
